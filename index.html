Technology

Fiber Loop Photonics — a 4‑step optical pipeline

ALTA replaces heavy, power-hungry electronic subsystems with a continuous optical path from sensor to downlink. No EMI. Nanosecond decisions. Mass and watts saved by orders of magnitude.

01

Sensing

Fiber Bragg Gratings (FBG)

Rad-hard, passive optical sensors embedded in the structural fiber continuously monitor thermal, vibration, and radiation events across the bus — with no moving parts and zero power draw.

All-optical anomaly detection at light speed — energy scales O(1) vs. O(N²) digital (Wang et al., 2025). A fully analogue readout eliminates A/D conversion, keeping the signal in the optical domain end-to-end (Duport et al., 2016).

Grounded in peer-reviewed science

  • Wang et al. (2025) — Optical next-generation reservoir computing, Nature Photonics. Demonstrates that energy consumption scales as O(1) versus O(N²) for digital counterparts; achieves NRMSE of 0.0971 on chaotic forecasting with 10× less training data.
  • Duport et al. (2012) — All-optical reservoir computing, Optics Express. First practical demonstration of all-optical reservoir computing using off-the-shelf fiber components, proving parity with state-of-the-art digital implementations.
  • Duport et al. (2016) — Fully analogue photonic reservoir computer, Scientific Reports. First demonstration of a complete end-to-end analogue pipeline — analogue optical input, analogue electrical output — eliminating the A/D conversion bottleneck and proving the full system can operate without any digital pre/post-processing.
